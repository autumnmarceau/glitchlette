##The GLITCHLETTE Universe Expands...
<center><figure><img class="blog-post-image" src="/glambient.JPG"><br>
Hello all! 

It’s been a while. I’m ramping back up to launch a new GLITCHLETTE era by releasing a bunch of old songs. They’re demos from up to nine years ago that I’ll never finish or release in any “official” capacity. Some of them go as far back as when I started producing music. Many of the mixes are pretty terrible, but some of them are kind of bangers, and I’d hate for them to sit on my hard drive. I mean, I went seven years without releasing new music, EVERY RAT alone isn’t gonna cut it! 

Some of my favorites are “Evaporator (3.19),”  “grey of gentler eves (10.18),” and “crashcrashcrash (10.21)”. 

I do indeed have new music coming (I sent the mix out to the mastering engineer today!). It’s certainly more “punk rock” than any of my previous stuff and, believe it or not, there are ZERO belted high notes. I know, I know…who have I become?!?

And speaking of punk rock, you may have heard, but I joined a band! Beautiful Freaks and I go way back to the Bellingham music scene. We would get booked together a lot (despite sounding totally different. I think bookers just didn’t know what to do with us). So far, it’s been a fun challenge to make music in a totally different genre. 

But never fear! This doesn’t mean I’m taking a break from GLITCHLETTE. Far from it! To be honest, I’ve been feeling uninspired lately. I’m excited to see how this collaboration will influence my sound. 

HAIL THE RAT LORD, 

Glitch
