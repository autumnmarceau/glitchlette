ABOUT GLITCHLETTE



Neuroplastic Pop: A post-genre synaptic upgrade.

GLITCHLETTE is a sonic project with a sound that is firmly rooted in pop sensibilities but not confined by them. Her sonic ambiguity avoids categorization by occupying the liminal spaces between genres. By evoking her diverse influences of progressive metal, trip hop, IDM and ambient, GLITCHLETTE crafts an original sound that is divisive, emotionally impactful and unmistakably her own.

GLITCHLETTE’s music is not for the background — it demands engagement. Her cinematic and non-linear song structures swerve between ethereal crescendos and crushing bass drops, more akin to scene changes in a film than traditional pop structure. At the heart of her sonic multiverse is her voice, a versatile, three octave instrument she commands to create theatrical hooks and ambient whispers.

Her 2018 debut EP Subsoil is an ethereal and maximalist pop record with intricate, shimmering production and danceable beats.

In 2020 she remixed and provided additional vocals to Default Gender's checking in with the old gang., which was well-received for its innovative additions to the original track.

Her upcoming sophomore EP, EVERY RAT WANTS TO CRAWL IN YOUR MOUTH AND CLEAN YOUR TEETH, set to release October 17, 2025, is a raw and unfiltered exploration of manic love, heartbreak, and catharsis.

EVERY RAT borrows from the language of cinema: tension and release, tonal shifts and left-turn plot twists. Though softer in sound than a typical slasher soundtrack, EVERY RAT carries the emotional viscera and ethos of psychological horror: the monster is never seen, only felt.
