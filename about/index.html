ABOUT GLITCHLETTE



Neuroplastic Pop

GLITCHLETTE is a sonic project with a sound that is firmly rooted in pop sensibilities but not confined by them. Her sonic ambiguity avoids categorization by occupying the spaces between genres, evoking her diverse influences of progressive metal, trip hop, IDM and ambient to create a sound that is divisive, emotionally impactful and unmistakably her own.

GLITCHLETTE’s music is not for the background — it demands engagement. Cinematic and irregular song structures swerve between ethereal crescendos and crushing bass drops, more akin to scene changes in a film than traditional pop structure. At the heart of her sonic multiverse is her voice, a three octave instrument she manipulates into theatrical hooks and ambient whispers.

GLITCHLETTE is neuroplastic pop: listen to install the post-genre synaptic upgrade.
