Bio

GLITCHLETTE is a genre-defying producer, composer, and vocalist from the Pacific Northwest who merges classical roots, harsh noise influences, and ethereal powerhouse vocals into spectral electronic pop. After over a decade as a classically trained cellist, she fell into her local experimental metal and noise scene. Inspired, she taught herself music production to unite her polar musical identities to form her own unique sound. In her world, left-field pop mutates into intricate orchestral swells and progressive drum beats, glued together by her three-octave mezzo soprano. The result is emotionally resonant and exploratory pop that’s familiar in feeling even when it’s unfamiliar in form.


After debuting with the 2018 EP SUBSOIL, she collaborated with Default Genders in 2020 on a remix of their track "Checking In With The Old Gang," which received acclaim from Pitchfork. In October 2025, GLITCHLETTE will showcase the evolution of her sound in her next EP: EVERY RAT WANTS TO CRAWL IN YOUR MOUTH AND CLEAN YOUR TEETH, a tale of classic heartbreak with an experimental twist. Written partially during 2020 quarantine and spiritually inspired by arthouse horror films and the postmodern novel House of Leaves, EVERY RAT filters emotional collapse through seismic sonic shifts, where soundscapes mutate from ethereal adagios into explosive breakdowns that cohere through GLITCHLETTE’s dynamic vocal hooks.
