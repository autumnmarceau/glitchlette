GLITCHLETTE is a cinematic and experimental pop project that weaves emotionally visceral electronic soundscapes and ethereal vocals into ghostly ballads and explosive anthems.

Since 2017, GLITCHLETTE has been the moniker of Autumn Marceau, a self-taught producer, vocalist, and songwriter. GLITCHLETTE's music exclusively features her own production, composition, and vocals, crafting songs that swing between intimate ballads and explosive, beat-driven breakdowns.

After debuting with the 2018 EP SUBSOIL, she collaborated with Default Genders in 2020. In September 2025, GLITCHLETTE will showcase the evolution of her sound in her sophomore EP: EVERY RAT WANTS TO CRAWL IN YOUR MOUTH AND CLEAN YOUR TEETH , a sonic diary of manic love, the psychological horror of heartbreak, and catharsis in spades.
